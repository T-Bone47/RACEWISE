## DMCA Compliance

### For Copyright Holders

If you believe content indexed by this aggregator infringes your copyright:

1. **Contact the source site directly** - Links point to third-party sites (dlpsgame.com, superpsx.com, etc.)
2. **Submit DMCA to source** - The hosting site controls the actual files
3. **Notify us** - We'll remove the link from our index upon valid notice

### Our DMCA Process

1. Receive valid DMCA notice at legal@yourdomain.com
2. Verify notice meets 17 USC § 512(c)(3) requirements
3. Remove link from index within 24 hours
4. Notify user who submitted the link (if applicable)
5. Maintain records for 10+ years

### Counter-Notification

If your link was removed in error:

1. Submit counter-notification per 17 USC § 512(g)
2. We'll restore link within 10-14 business days
3. Original complainant must file suit to keep link down
